Independent security review, including AI-assisted inspection, remains permitted under [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [AI_POLICY.md](AI_POLICY.md). Unchanged local audit clones and reproducible builds are allowed; source modifications by other users are not. The restrictions concern training and unauthorized product reuse; they do not require favorable findings or prohibit reporting defects.

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately using [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/battlejoose/soltoshidice-programs/security/advisories/new).

Include the affected program address and network, a description of the issue, its potential impact, and reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept. Use local fixtures or the documented devnet environment for testing. Do not move another user's funds, disrupt production, or publish exploitation details before coordinated disclosure.

Never include private keys, seed phrases, access tokens, or other credentials. Do not use public GitHub issues for undisclosed vulnerabilities.

The current mainnet program identities, source revisions, and deployed-binary hashes are listed in `programs.json`. Source verification establishes a match between source and deployed code; it is not a security audit or a guarantee that the programs are free of vulnerabilities.

No bounty or response-time guarantee is offered by this policy.

For general support or private off-chain data requests, see [Support and privacy requests](SUPPORT.md).
